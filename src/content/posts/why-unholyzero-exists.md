Most developer blogs begin with a framework choice. UnholyZero begins with a quality bar.

The internet has enough tutorials that repeat the documentation, enough AI-generated summaries that say nothing, and enough confident advice that was never tested. This site will focus on the opposite: small experiments, clear trade-offs, useful code, and the occasional joke when the build finally passes.

AI can help with research, structure, and editing. It cannot be the excuse for publishing a post that nobody would bookmark. Every article here should earn its place by helping a developer make a decision or avoid a mistake.
